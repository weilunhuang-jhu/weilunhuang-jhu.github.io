Wei-Lun Huang

Computer Scientist

I am a researcher at Lumo Imaging, focusing on computer vision, graphics, and robotics for 2D/3D total body photography. I am also actively contributing to DICOM community in WG19 Dermatology.

Previously, I pursued my PhD in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, where I work with Mehran Armand, Misha Kazhdan, and Joshua Liu. During my PhD, I also worked as a pre-doctoral visiting fellow at NICHD supervised by Amir Gandjbakhche. Before my PhD, I received my M.S.E. in Robotics from Johns Hopkins University and my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and B.A. in Economics from National Taiwan University. Some of my undergraduate fun projects can be found here.

During my free time, I enjoy playing music.
